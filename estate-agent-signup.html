Refer buyers and sellers and earn on every completion
FindConveyancers helps Partners connect their clients with regulated local conveyancers. You earn a referral fee for every matter that completes through your link, paid on completion.
How it works
1

You share your unique referral link with buyers and sellers

We give you a personalised link to pass on at the point a sale is agreed. You can share it by email, in person, or on your website, however suits you.

2

They submit their details and receive quotes from local conveyancers

Your client fills in a quick form and receives fee quotes from regulated local firms. The comparison is free and takes under two minutes.

3

They select a firm and instruct them

The client chooses the conveyancer they want and instructs them directly through the platform. No paperwork on your end.

4

The matter completes, you receive your referral fee

Once the transaction completes, your referral fee is paid.
