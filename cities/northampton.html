Frequently Asked Questions

How long does conveyancing take in Northampton? +

Typical timeline is 8-12 weeks from offer to completion. Northampton Council typically responds to searches within 10-15 days, which is faster than many UK councils.

What's included in conveyancing fees? +

Standard fees cover: document review, searches, enquiries, contract preparation, lender liaison, and Land Registry registration. Always confirm what's included in your quote.

Can I use an online conveyancer? +

Yes. Online conveyancers are often 20-30% cheaper than high street solicitors and equally qualified. Choose one with good reviews and that offers fixed fees for transparency.

What if my survey finds problems? +

You can renegotiate the price, request repairs, or walk away (if before contracts are exchanged). Your conveyancer will advise on the best course of action.
