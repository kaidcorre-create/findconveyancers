Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about conveyancing

What is conveyancing? ▼

Conveyancing is the legal process of transferring property ownership from the seller to the buyer. A conveyancer (solicitor or licensed conveyancer) handles all the paperwork and legal work.

How much does conveyancing cost? ▼

The average cost is £1,624 for buyers and £1,023 for sellers in 2026. However, prices vary based on property type and location. Get quotes to see exact prices.

Are all conveyancers regulated? ▼

All conveyancers on FindConveyancers are regulated by either the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA) or the Council for Licensed Conveyancers (CLC).

How long does conveyancing take? ▼

The typical timescale is 8-12 weeks from offer to completion, though this can be faster with an efficient solicitor.

What if my sale falls through? ▼

Many solicitors offer "no sale, no fee" arrangements, though disbursements (searches, etc.) are typically non-refundable. Check the terms when you choose your conveyancer.

Do I need a conveyancer if I'm remortgaging? ▼

Yes, your lender will require a conveyancer to handle the legal work. Remortgage conveyancing is simpler and typically costs £400-£800.

Is my data secure? ▼

Yes. We use ISO 27001 certified security, encrypted data transmission, and never share your information without consent.

Can I change solicitor mid-transaction? ▼

Yes, you can change solicitors, though it may delay completion. It's best to get it right the first time by comparing quotes upfront.
