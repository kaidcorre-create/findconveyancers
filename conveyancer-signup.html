How FindConveyancers works
Everything you need to know before registering — read this first.
Step 1 — Receiving leads

We send you the lead by email

When a client submits an enquiry that matches your region and transaction type, we send their details to your nominated email address straight away.

Respond within 24 hours

You are required to contact the client within 24 hours of receiving the lead. Speed matters — clients on our platform are comparing multiple firms.

Step 2 — Submitting your quote

Submit your fee quote via a private link

Each lead email includes a unique link to our quoting portal. Enter your legal fee, VAT, and disbursements. The client sees a clear breakdown and compares your quote against others.

Client chooses and instructs you directly

If the client selects your quote, they instruct you through the platform. You then handle the matter in the usual way — we play no further role in the transaction.

Step 3 — Reporting completion & paying

Let us know when the matter completes

Within 5 business days of completion, notify us by email. We may independently verify completion against HM Land Registry records.

Pay on completion — nothing upfront

Our completion fee is only due once the transaction completes. There are no monthly fees, no subscription costs, and no charge for leads that don't convert.

No risk to join. Registration is free. You only pay when a matter completes — and only for matters that came through FindConveyancers. Completion fees are agreed in the supply agreement you'll review on the next step.

Referral Supply Agreement
Please read the agreement below, then tick to confirm your acceptance before submitting.

1. The service

FindConveyancers (operated by Corre Connections) will supply you with conveyancing referrals — consumer enquiries matched to your geographic area and transaction types. Each referral consists of the client's contact details, property information, and transaction type.

2. Pay on completion

This agreement operates on a pay-per-completion basis. No fee is due on delivery of a referral, and no fee is due if a referred matter does not proceed to completion. The completion fee is only payable once the conveyancing transaction legally completes.

The agreed completion fee will be confirmed in writing before your account is activated. Fees are exclusive of VAT.

3. Responding to leads

You agree to contact each referred client within 24 hours of receiving their details. Failure to respond promptly may result in suspension from the platform.

4. Reporting completion

You must notify FindConveyancers of each completion within 5 business days of the completion date. We may independently verify completions via HM Land Registry records. Failure to notify may result in late payment interest being applied.

5. Your obligations

  • You must hold a current practising certificate or licence from the SRA or CLC at all times.
  • You must maintain adequate professional indemnity insurance.
  • You must comply with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 in handling client data.
  • You must not represent yourself as affiliated with or endorsed by FindConveyancers.
  • You must deal with clients professionally and in accordance with your regulatory obligations.

6. What we warrant

We warrant that each referral delivered will contain genuine contact details submitted by a real client, relate to a genuine conveyancing enquiry, and not be a duplicate of a referral previously delivered to you for the same property within the preceding 6 months.

We do not warrant that any referral will result in an instruction or that any transaction will proceed to completion.

7. Rejection of referrals

You may reject a referral and request a credit within 5 business days of delivery if: the client's contact details are demonstrably non-functional; the referral is a verified duplicate; or the referral falls outside the geographic area or transaction types you have specified.

8. Liability

FindConveyancers is not responsible for the quality or outcome of legal services you provide. Our total liability to you under this agreement shall not exceed the amount of fees paid by you in the 3 months preceding the claim.

9. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement on 30 days' written notice. We may terminate immediately if you lose your SRA or CLC authorisation, or commit a material breach that is not remedied within 14 days of notice.

10. Data protection

Each party is an independent data controller. We will share client personal data with you solely for the purpose of providing conveyancing services to that client. You must not use the data for any other purpose and must comply with all applicable data protection legislation.

11. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
